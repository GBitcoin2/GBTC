### 5.1 Public Circulation and Trading

**Allocation: 18,000,000 GBTC (85.71%)**

This allocation is designated for public circulation and trading.

It represents the largest portion of the token distribution and is intended to support the token's availability within the market.

### 5.2 Team Allocation

**Allocation: 1,000,000 GBTC (4.76%)**

This allocation is designated for the GBitcoin project team to support project development, coordination, and ongoing ecosystem operations.

### 5.3 GBTC Ecosystem Project Support

**Allocation: 2,000,000 GBTC (9.52%)**

This allocation is designated to support projects that are part of, associated with, or developed within the GBitcoin ecosystem.

Its intended purpose is to encourage ecosystem development, support connected initiatives, and help expand the range of projects and services associated with GBitcoin.

This allocation is specifically intended for projects related to the GBTC ecosystem rather than unrelated external projects.

## 6. Ecosystem Vision

GBTC aims to support the development of an interconnected ecosystem where projects can maintain their individual identities while exploring shared infrastructure, liquidity opportunities, trading-pair integrations, and additional utility.

The ecosystem project-support allocation is intended to contribute to this broader development vision.

- Allocation figures describe the intended distribution and do not establish locking, vesting, or release schedules.
- Any future changes to token distribution or allocation policies should be communicated through official project channels.
