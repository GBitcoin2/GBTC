## 01. Our Foundation

GBitcoin (GBTC) is built on the vision of bringing multiple independent digital assets and projects together within one interconnected ecosystem.

At the heart of this vision is GBTC — the core asset that connects participating projects and their tokens through a shared framework of liquidity, trading relationships, and ecosystem collaboration.

Each participating asset retains its own identity, purpose, and individual direction while becoming part of a broader structure designed to encourage interaction, strengthen connections, and support shared ecosystem development.

GBTC represents the common foundation of this model: bringing different assets together under one ecosystem vision without removing what makes each project unique.

We believe that meaningful growth comes from stronger connections, productive cooperation, and an ecosystem in which multiple projects can develop alongside one another.

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of respect, integrity, responsibility, and collaboration expected from everyone participating in the GBTC ecosystem.

## 02. Ecosystem Principles

The GBTC ecosystem is guided by a shared vision of connection, cooperation, and long-term development.

Its fundamental principles include:

- **Unity Through Connection:** Bringing multiple projects and digital assets together within one interconnected ecosystem.
- **Individual Identity:** Respecting the independent identity, purpose, and direction of every participating project and asset.
- **Shared Infrastructure:** Encouraging connections through liquidity pools, trading pairs, and complementary ecosystem resources.
- **Collaborative Development:** Supporting meaningful interaction and cooperation between participating projects.
- **Shared Growth:** Creating an environment where connected projects can develop alongside one another.
- **Long-Term Vision:** Building a sustainable ecosystem through continuous improvement and responsible development.

These principles define the direction of GBTC and provide a foundation for collaboration across its ecosystem.

## 03. Principles of Participation

Participation in the GBTC project and its wider ecosystem should be guided by the following standards:

- **Respect:** Treat every participant with dignity, regardless of experience, background, or technical knowledge.
- **Integrity:** Communicate honestly and represent information accurately.
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for personal actions, statements, and contributions.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus on solutions, meaningful feedback, and improvements.
- **Transparency:** Avoid misleading claims, concealed intentions, and misrepresentation.
- **Professionalism:** Maintain a standard of communication appropriate for a serious technology project.

These principles apply to all forms of participation, whether technical, community-related, or ecosystem-focused.

## 04. Community Standards

All participants are expected to contribute to a productive and respectful environment.

This includes:

- Engaging in discussions with courtesy and good judgment.
- Presenting opinions and criticism in a clear and constructive manner.
- Respecting different viewpoints and allowing room for reasonable disagreement.
- Sharing information responsibly and distinguishing verified facts from personal opinions.
- Recognizing the efforts and contributions of others.
- Avoiding unnecessary conflict, provocation, and disruptive behavior.
- Maintaining respectful communication even when disagreements arise.
- Respecting the identity, objectives, and contributions of different projects within the ecosystem.

The quality of an ecosystem is reflected not only in its technical connections, but also in how its participants communicate, cooperate, and respect one another.

## 05. Conduct That Is Not Accepted

The following behaviors are inconsistent with the standards of the GBTC ecosystem:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Threats, personal attacks, or deliberately hostile communication.
- Publishing private information without authorization.
- Impersonating project representatives or presenting unofficial statements as official.
- Knowingly spreading false or deceptive information about GBTC, participating projects, or their contributors.
- Misrepresenting partnerships, integrations, project relationships, or ecosystem developments.
- Manipulating discussions or contributions through dishonest practices.
- Deliberately disrupting project activities or community interactions.
- Disrespecting the identity, contributions, or legitimate interests of other participating projects.
- Retaliating against individuals for expressing concerns in good faith.

Such conduct undermines trust, damages cooperation, and has no place in a professional ecosystem.

## 06. Scope of Application

This Code applies to interactions connected with GBitcoin and its ecosystem, including:

- Official GBTC GitHub repositories and project discussions.
- Development contributions and technical collaboration.
- Official project and community communication channels.
- Ecosystem-related announcements and public discussions.
- Interactions involving participating projects and their representatives.
- Other environments where individuals act on behalf of, or directly represent, GBTC.

Participants are expected to maintain these standards wherever their actions are associated with the project or its ecosystem.

## 07. Disagreement & Constructive Criticism

GBTC recognizes that different opinions, technical approaches, project strategies, and critical perspectives are a natural part of ecosystem development.

Constructive criticism is welcome. Questions, independent research, technical analysis, and respectful disagreement should not be discouraged.

Participating projects may have different objectives, development priorities, and approaches. These differences should be respected as part of an ecosystem that brings distinct projects and assets together.

Discussions should remain focused on facts, reasoning, and relevant ideas rather than personal disputes or unnecessary conflict.

**A difference of opinion is not misconduct. Disrespectful behavior is.**

## 08. Trust & Responsibility

Trust is developed through consistent actions, honest communication, and respect for shared standards.

Every participant has a responsibility to protect the quality of project-related interactions and avoid behavior that damages confidence between community members and participating projects.

Contributors and community members should exercise good judgment when sharing information, discussing ecosystem developments, representing project relationships, or communicating in an official capacity.

The strength of an interconnected ecosystem depends on responsible participation and respect for the different projects that contribute to its development.

## 09. Maintaining These Standards

Conduct that conflicts with this Code may be subject to appropriate action within project-managed spaces.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

- A formal warning.
- Removal of inappropriate material.
- Restrictions on participation.
- Removal from project-managed spaces.

Any response should consider the nature of the conduct, its impact, and the surrounding circumstances.

The purpose of these standards is to maintain a respectful and productive environment, protect constructive collaboration, and preserve the integrity of the GBTC ecosystem.

These standards are not intended to prevent legitimate criticism, independent discussion, or reasonable differences between participating projects.

## 10. Commitment to Continuous Improvement

The GBTC ecosystem is intended to evolve through the development of new connections, participating projects, digital assets, and shared opportunities.

As the ecosystem grows, its community practices and collaboration standards may also develop.

This Code of Conduct may be revised when necessary to reflect the project's direction and maintain clear expectations for participation.

All participants are encouraged to uphold these principles and contribute to an ecosystem built on respect, responsibility, meaningful cooperation, and a shared vision of development.
