
# GBitcoin Roadmap

## Overview

The GBitcoin (GBTC) roadmap outlines the project's long-term vision and development direction within the GRAM ecosystem.

Our objective is to develop an interconnected environment that brings independent projects, digital assets, liquidity infrastructure, and practical services together through shared opportunities and collaboration.

The roadmap represents development intentions and priorities. It does not guarantee that every proposed feature will be implemented or launched within a specific timeframe.

## Phase 1 — Project Foundation

Focus on establishing the project's identity, initial infrastructure, and public documentation.

Key objectives:

- Establish the GBitcoin project identity and branding.
- Introduce the GBTC token within the GRAM ecosystem.
- Develop and publish the official project website.
- Establish public documentation and communication channels.
- Define the project's multi-project ecosystem vision.
- Establish transparency, security, and documentation principles.

## Phase 2 — Ecosystem Development

Focus on developing the foundations for connecting independent projects and digital assets.

Key objectives:

- Explore opportunities for collaboration with independent projects.
- Identify suitable assets and potential ecosystem participants.
- Develop the architecture for interconnected project infrastructure.
- Explore liquidity pool and trading-pair integrations.
- Establish clear participation and integration principles.

## Phase 3 — Liquidity Infrastructure

Focus on expanding the ecosystem's liquidity-related capabilities.

Key objectives:

- Explore multi-project liquidity arrangements.
- Support potential trading-pair integrations.
- Improve connectivity between participating assets.
- Evaluate infrastructure for liquidity management.
- Encourage responsible and transparent liquidity practices.

Liquidity availability, depth, and performance will depend on actual integrations, market conditions, and participating assets.

## Phase 4 — Utility and Service Expansion

Explore additional services that may expand the ecosystem beyond liquidity infrastructure.

Potential development areas include:

- Financial and digital asset services.
- Asset management and related tools.
- Rewards and incentive mechanisms.
- Educational resources and services.
- Project integration and collaboration tools.
- Additional applications across different digital sectors.

These areas represent potential development directions and are not claims of currently available functionality.

## Phase 5 — Ecosystem Expansion

Focus on broader adoption, interoperability, and long-term ecosystem development.

Key objectives:

- Expand collaboration with additional independent projects.
- Explore new integrations and infrastructure opportunities.
- Encourage participation from developers and communities.
- Evaluate additional utility models and service applications.
- Strengthen the interconnected ecosystem over time.

## Development Principles

GBitcoin's development approach is guided by the following principles:

- **Transparency:** Communicate development progress and important changes clearly.
- **Independence:** Respect the identity and economic independence of connected projects.
- **Collaboration:** Encourage meaningful cooperation between ecosystem participants.
- **Practical Utility:** Prioritize useful infrastructure and sustainable applications.
- **Security:** Consider technical security and risk management throughout development.
- **Responsible Growth:** Avoid unsupported promises and communicate limitations honestly.

## Roadmap Updates

This roadmap may be revised as the project develops, new opportunities emerge, and technical priorities change.

Completed milestones should only be marked as completed after the relevant work has been implemented and verified.
