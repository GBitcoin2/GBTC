## Our Appreciation

The development of GBitcoin (GBTC) is driven by a long-term vision of building an interconnected ecosystem where independent projects and digital assets can participate in a shared environment.

We recognize the value of every idea, technical insight, constructive discussion, and contribution that helps move this vision forward.

## The Open-Source Spirit

Open-source development has played an important role in expanding access to technology and encouraging knowledge sharing across the global developer community.

We appreciate the developers, researchers, and communities whose work helps make technological progress more accessible and collaborative.

## Innovation & Technical Progress

Building an interconnected digital asset ecosystem requires continuous learning, careful development, and a commitment to improving the underlying infrastructure.

We value the technical knowledge, development tools, research, and innovative approaches that contribute to the broader evolution of blockchain technology.

## The GRAM Ecosystem

GBTC's initial development direction is connected to the GRAM ecosystem.

We acknowledge the wider ecosystem of builders, developers, projects, and participants whose ongoing efforts contribute to its growth and development.

## Community & Engagement

A strong project benefits from an informed and engaged community.

We appreciate everyone who follows GBTC, explores its vision, shares thoughtful feedback, identifies potential improvements, and participates in meaningful discussions.

Every constructive contribution helps create opportunities for continued development.

## Shared Vision

GBTC is built around the idea of connecting multiple projects and digital assets through interconnected liquidity and shared infrastructure.

Our appreciation extends to everyone who believes in collaboration, innovation, and the potential of connected digital ecosystems.

## Looking Ahead

As the GBTC ecosystem develops, this document may evolve to reflect meaningful contributions, milestones, community initiatives, and progress achieved along the way.

We believe that sustainable development is an ongoing process shaped by ideas, experience, and continuous improvement.
