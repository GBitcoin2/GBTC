# ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

### GBitcoin · GBTC

**Recognizing the People, Technologies, and Communities Behind Progress**

*Built on knowledge. Strengthened through collaboration. Driven by innovation.*

</div>

---

## 01 — Purpose

The GBitcoin (GBTC) project recognizes that meaningful technological development is built upon the collective knowledge, creativity, and contributions of individuals and communities across the digital ecosystem.

This document serves as an official expression of appreciation to the people, developers, open-source communities, technology providers, and contributors whose work, expertise, and resources help make continued development possible.

We believe that acknowledging the foundations behind innovation is an essential part of responsible project development.

## 02 — Open-Source Community

We extend our sincere appreciation to the global open-source community.

Open-source software plays an important role in advancing technology by making knowledge, tools, and technical resources accessible to developers around the world.

The work of open-source contributors helps create opportunities for experimentation, collaboration, transparency, and continuous improvement.

GBTC recognizes the dedication of developers and maintainers who contribute their time and expertise to building and improving the technologies that support the broader digital ecosystem.

## 03 — Developers & Technical Contributors

We acknowledge the developers, engineers, researchers, and technical contributors whose knowledge and work help advance blockchain technology and digital infrastructure.

Their contributions across software development, technical research, security practices, documentation, and engineering provide valuable foundations for innovation.

We also appreciate individuals who contribute through:

- Technical reviews and constructive feedback.
- Software testing and issue reporting.
- Documentation improvements.
- Development suggestions and research.
- Identification of potential security concerns.
- Technical discussions and knowledge sharing.

Every meaningful contribution helps improve the quality of the development process.

## 04 — Technologies & Infrastructure

The development of GBTC may involve the use of third-party technologies, development frameworks, software libraries, infrastructure tools, and digital services.

We recognize the creators and maintainers responsible for developing and maintaining these resources.

Third-party technologies remain the property of their respective owners and are governed by their applicable licenses, terms of service, and attribution requirements.

Where required, relevant copyright notices, license references, and attribution information should be maintained in accordance with the applicable terms.

Acknowledgment of a technology or service does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official affiliation.

## 05 — Blockchain Ecosystem

GBTC acknowledges the broader blockchain ecosystem and the developers, communities, infrastructure providers, and technical contributors working to advance blockchain technology.

We recognize the importance of the tools, protocols, networks, and supporting infrastructure that enable digital assets and blockchain-based applications to evolve.

The project's initial development direction is associated with the GRAM ecosystem.

Recognition of an ecosystem or its technologies does not imply an official partnership or endorsement unless explicitly confirmed.

## 06 — Community & Feedback

A project's development benefits from meaningful communication and constructive participation.

We appreciate everyone who follows GBTC, explores its development, shares thoughtful feedback, reports issues, and contributes ideas that may help improve the project.

Community participation can take many forms, including:

- Sharing constructive suggestions.
- Identifying documentation inaccuracies.
- Reporting technical issues.
- Discussing ecosystem opportunities.
- Supporting transparent communication.
- Encouraging responsible development practices.

We value contributions that promote technical quality, clarity, and long-term progress.

## 07 — Recognition of Contributions

We believe that contributions should be acknowledged fairly and transparently.

Recognition may be given to individuals or organizations whose contributions are verified and relevant to the project.

Contributions may include software development, technical research, testing, security reviews, documentation, design, and other forms of meaningful project support.

Any formal recognition should respect the contributor's authorship, applicable intellectual property rights, and stated preferences regarding attribution.

Being acknowledged does not automatically imply employment, ownership, an official project role, or a formal partnership.

## 08 — Third-Party Attribution & Licensing

GBTC respects intellectual property rights and the licensing requirements associated with external resources.

Third-party software, libraries, frameworks, visual assets, documentation, and other materials may be subject to separate ownership and licensing conditions.

The project aims to:

- Respect applicable open-source licenses.
- Preserve required copyright notices.
- Provide attribution where required.
- Avoid implying ownership of third-party materials.
- Respect the terms governing external tools and services.

Where applicable, specific attribution details should be documented alongside the relevant component or within the project's licensing documentation.

## 09 — Independence & Transparency

Acknowledgments are intended to recognize contributions and express appreciation.

Unless explicitly stated and independently confirmed, acknowledgment must not be interpreted as evidence of:

- Official partnership or sponsorship.
- Commercial endorsement.
- Ownership or control of third-party technologies.
- Employment or contractual relationships.
- Investment or financial support.
- Formal affiliation with an external organization.

GBTC aims to maintain clear distinctions between appreciation, technical usage, community participation, and formal project relationships.

## 10 — Continuous Recognition

As the GBTC project evolves, this document may be updated to recognize additional contributors, technologies, resources, and communities whose contributions become relevant to the project.

Recognition should remain accurate, appropriate, and consistent with the rights and preferences of the individuals and organizations concerned.

We believe that transparency and proper attribution are essential to maintaining trust and respecting the work of others.
