
# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is GBitcoin?

GBitcoin (GBTC) is a project focused on developing a multi-project ecosystem on the GRAM network, with an emphasis on connecting independent projects, digital assets, and services through shared infrastructure.

## 2. What is the GBTC token?

GBTC is the token associated with the GBitcoin project.

- **Project Name:** GBitcoin
- **Token Symbol:** GBTC
- **Network:** GRAM
- **Decimals:** 9
- **Declared Total Supply:** 21,000,000 GBTC

## 3. What is the main vision of GBitcoin?

The vision is to build an interconnected ecosystem where independent projects can retain their own identities and purposes while exploring opportunities for collaboration, shared infrastructure, liquidity, and broader utility.

## 4. How does the multi-project concept work?

The concept focuses on connecting different projects and digital assets through potential shared infrastructure, liquidity pools, and trading pairs.

Each participating project remains independent. Connections between assets do not guarantee price stability, liquidity, financial support, or protection against losses.

## 5. Is GBitcoin a decentralized exchange?

GBitcoin's broader vision includes interconnected liquidity and trading infrastructure. However, any exchange-related functionality should be considered available only when it has been officially developed, deployed, and announced.

## 6. Which network does GBitcoin use?

The GBTC token is associated with the GRAM network.

Always verify the network and token contract before interacting with digital assets.

Availability depends on supported platforms, liquidity, and officially announced integrations.

## 9. Does GBitcoin guarantee profits or token value?

No. GBitcoin does not guarantee profits, price appreciation, liquidity, or financial returns.

## 10. Are connected projects financially backed by GBTC?

Not automatically. Connecting projects through shared infrastructure or liquidity arrangements does not mean that one token guarantees, backs, or protects another token.

Each asset retains its own economic characteristics and risks.
